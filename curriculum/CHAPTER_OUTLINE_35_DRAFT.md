# Tablinum — 35-chapter course outline (approval draft)

**Prepared 4 November 2026.** Content-planning only. **No existing JavaScript, lessons, scene assets, audio or learner state is modified.** This is based on the uploaded `TABLINUM_HANDOVER_FOR_GPT(1).md`, with its explicit instruction to gain approval before writing new lessons.

## Design commitments

- Keep the existing original cast (Mārcus, Flāvia, Līvia, Tītus and Canis) and classical manuscript UI.
- Preserve Chapters I–VI narrative topics; split the old Chapter VII storm and Chapter VIII future content into smaller, comprehensible steps. Existing chapters are not rewritten until this outline is approved.
- Latin-first, illustrations with semantic colours, word-click glosses, and English only on request. Content and picture notes are *not yet certified by a Latinist*.
- Aim for **30–60 original Latin sentences per chapter**, including predictable scenes and 8–10 meaningful recurrences of an important newly introduced word, with review readings roughly every four chapters.
- One main new grammatical idea per teaching chapter; review chapters add no unprepared construction. Chapter IV also gets a short review vignette to give the first review after four chapters.
- Maintain a small local-first runtime; future grammar/lemma checks and audio alignment must run offline before publishing JSON assets.
- Each non-review chapter should have a checked main picture, Latin-only margin notes, a short 5–10 item Latin drill, and a reread step. A user-approved character sheet is required before replacement art is generated.

## Structural progression

- **I–VIII:** Introducing people, places and direct actions
- **IX–XVI:** Past experiences and future plans
- **XVII–XX:** Cases, reference and relative clauses
- **XXI–XXVIII:** Narrating completed events and passive voice
- **XXIX–XXXV:** Participles, basic subjunctive, noun families and independent reading

## Important vocabulary and scale decision

The handover requests **800–1,200 known words**, a 35-chapter course, and a warning at about **15 new lemmas per chapter**. Those goals conflict mathematically. Even all 35 chapters at 15 new lemmas would contribute at most **525**; the present plan has **8 full review/bridge chapters**, leaving only 27 instructional chapters (a 405-lemma upper bound at 15 each). Existing lessons might already introduce several additional lemmas, but that does not close the overall gap. The 126 DCC references in this table are 95 distinct sample lemmas, **not** a claim of taught or mastered vocabulary.

**Proposed option for approval:** Make these 35 chapters a genuinely controlled *foundation reader* with no more than ~15 new lemmas in early chapters, gradually increasing only after comprehension evidence. Add graded supplementary readers and a subsequent intermediate course to reach 800–1,200 known lemmas. If 800–1,200 must be learned within the 35 chapters alone, the chapter length, allowable vocabulary load, and number of review lessons must be explicitly re-scoped rather than silently exceeding the rule.
