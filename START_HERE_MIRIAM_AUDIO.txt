LATIN X - MIRIAM AUDIO IN THE READER (9 November 2026)

1. Extract this whole ZIP into a new folder (keep your old folder; your saved
   progress lives in the browser and is not touched).
2. Double-click "Start Tablinum.cmd".
3. Open Chapter I (In horto). Press "Select / listen" on sentence 1.
   Miriam reads it. When a sentence ends, the reader moves to the next one and
   keeps reading while Miriam has a recording for it.
4. Click a word such as "magna" and press "Miriam" in the side panel to hear the word.

WHAT IS INCLUDED
- Chapter I sentences 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 8: Miriam, your accepted takes.
- Words cena, Gaius, magna, pecunia, laeta: Miriam, your accepted takes.
