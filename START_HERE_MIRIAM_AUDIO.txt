LATIN X - MIRIAM AUDIO IN THE READER (9 November 2026)

1. Extract this whole ZIP into a new folder (keep your old folder; your saved
   progress lives in the browser and is not touched).
2. Double-click "Start Tablinum.cmd".
3. Open Chapter I (In horto). Press "Select / listen" on sentence 1.
   Miriam reads it. When a sentence ends, the reader moves to the next one and
   keeps reading while Miriam has a recording for it.
4. Click a word such as "magna" and press "Miriam" in the side panel to hear the word.

WHAT IS INCLUDED
- Chapter I sentences 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 8: Miriam, your accepted takes.
- Words cena, Gaius, magna, pecunia, laeta: Miriam, your accepted takes.

WHAT IS NOT INCLUDED YET (approved, but the files could not be downloaded)
- Sentence 7 V6b (default) and V6a (alternative)
- Sentence 9 V5a
- Words quoque V4, villa V2, aqua V3
For these, the reader says so and uses the browser voice, labelled
"Browser voice - not Miriam". Nothing was regenerated or faked.

TO ADD THE MISSING SIX
1. While online, run AUDIO_OWNER_PACKAGE\DOWNLOAD_APPROVED_MP3S.cmd (sentence 7,
   sentence 9, quoque), plus DOWNLOAD_MIRIAM_IPA_TRIALS.cmd (villa V2) and
   DOWNLOAD_MIRIAM_ALIAS_V3.cmd (aqua V3) from this folder. If a link has expired,
   download the existing Runway task by its ID (listed in
   audio\narration\miriam\AUDIO_MANIFEST.json) - do not generate a new take.
2. Double-click "Install Approved Narration.cmd" and give it the folder holding
   the downloaded files. It checks each file is a real MP3 and prints its SHA-256.
   (It needs Node.js 22, like the other review tools.)
