Discere legendo · Learn by reading

Follow Līvia and her family through eight illustrated lessons. Read Latin at your own pace, listen to individual sentences and practise what you understand.

I · Lectiō — Read

Choose a chapter. Click a sentence to hear only that line. Click a word to look it up. Reveal Latin and English clues when you need help.

II · Exercitia — Practise

Use the questions and exercises to check your comprehension, revisit the reading and learn at your own pace.

III · Memoria — Remember

Save useful words and practise spaced recall. Progressus records your reading and practice history locally.

Progression

Read the chapters in order: I · Hortus, II · Familia, III · Dōnum, IV · Cēna, V · Forum, VI · Canis, VII · Tempestās and VIII · Crās. Earlier vocabulary builds a foundation for later chapters; you can revisit any lesson.
